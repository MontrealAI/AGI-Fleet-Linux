## Finite external gates

- An operator with a fresh supported Linux x64 or ARM64 machine records installation, separate accounts, Docker isolation, STOP recovery and reboot observations in the blank ledger. The next permitted action is controlled installation after exact-archive engineering acceptance.
- A provider-account owner separately authorizes bounded calls and captures sanitized receipts, actual cost and application competence. The next permitted action is the existing commissioning flow, never imported authority.
- A separately administered reviewer records identity and conflicts before claiming independent control. The software's `UNVERIFIED` label remains until that evidence exists.
- A human accessibility/novice tester runs the published EN/FR tasks with assistive technology and records difficulties. Automated axe results do not substitute for that session.
- An operator measures the required endurance interval and failures/recovery. No elapsed time is inferred from a short CI test.
