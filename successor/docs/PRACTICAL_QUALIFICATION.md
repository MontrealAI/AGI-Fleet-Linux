## Append-only attempt observations

Installed execution now records reserved, dispatched and blocked/uncertain attempts in the private `successor-outcomes/attempts.jsonl` store. STOP and result inspection add observations without executing another action. Repeated result downloads may add observations; they do not create a new attempt or multiply a charge. Result exports retain observations for that attempt. The ledger uses exclusive append locking, a hash chain, strict schemas, private regular files and fsync; torn history or an uncleared append lock fails closed. Preserve and reconcile evidence before repairing a damaged store. A hash chain is not external attestation.

Actual provider charges are **unknown** until billing evidence is recorded. Estimated, metered, invoiced, subscription-allocated and unknown values stay separate by currency; the ledger does not sum them as a fully loaded cost. Charge IDs reject accidental duplication. Active and waiting seconds are separate for setup, preparation, authorization, monitoring, review, repair, support and recovery. Record human observations with their evidence hash and `observed` or `operator-reported` basis:

```sh
node fleet/successor/outcome-cli.mjs --store /ABSOLUTE/PRIVATE/successor-outcomes --event observation.json
node fleet/successor/outcome-cli.mjs --store /ABSOLUTE/PRIVATE/successor-outcomes --summary
```

Each event has `attempt` (hash), `type`, `at` (milliseconds), `evidenceSha256` and typed `data`. See `outcomes.mjs` for the closed schema. Billing observations require a unique ID, category, basis, currency, exact nonnegative `amountMicros` string (or null when unknown), and billing-reference hash. Human-time observations require stage, activeSeconds, waitingSeconds and measurement basis. No payment or runtime authority follows from any record.

Failed, uncertain and abandoned attempts remain in the ledger. Review assertions do not establish eligibility: even externally attested entries require separate audit. Cost and active minutes per independently accepted outcome remain null while that audit and complete observations are absent. Allocate setup with a disclosed amortization; choose one economic basis for each expense and avoid double-counting billed review and reviewer labor.
