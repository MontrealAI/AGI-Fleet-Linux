Have these ready

  • A dedicated, clean Ubuntu desktop with GNOME, on x86-64 or ARM64.
  • At least 8 GB of memory (16 GB recommended) and 40 GB of free storage.
  • A reliable internet connection and the provider access needed for the prompted sign-ins.
  • An administrator who can prepare the computer. Existing Docker installations need a different clean host; do not remove one to force this setup.

Know the costs and timing

  • Exploring this guide does not cost anything.
  • Your computer, internet, electricity and AI provider usage can have costs. Set your approved budget before testing.
  • Plan a supervised installation, then 72 elapsed hours of live observation after initial checks.
  • Paid work and earnings are not guaranteed. Keep funding and transactions off during qualification.
What setup involves — for you and your helper
Three accounts, with clear jobs. Your existing administrator prepares the computer. A new approval account (called signer) protects the wallet and approvals. A separate new work account (called compute) does the AI work. The setup helper creates the two new accounts.
  1. Administrator

    Download, check and prepare

    Download the complete Fleet ZIP and its checksum file from the same release. Follow the guide to verify the ZIP, extract it and create the two fresh accounts. Continue only after the required checks pass.

  2. Signer account

    Create your private setup kit

    Sign in through Ubuntu’s login screen. Open the role’s local app, create a new qualification wallet, protect its recovery information and export the setup kit. Keep the signer stopped after export. Never paste recovery words or the private setup kit into this website or a support message.

  3. Administrator

    Transfer only the setup kit

    Use the supplied transfer helper to copy the kit to the compute account. The wallet stays in the signer account. Do not copy the whole signer home.

  4. Compute account

    Install and connect the AI tools

    Sign in through Ubuntu’s login screen. Follow the guide’s compute preparation and installation steps, then review the policy before approving it. Follow the separate Creator, Checker and Reviewer sign-ins described in the guide.

  5. Compute and signer accounts · follow the guide

    Test, reboot and keep a record

    Check authenticated health, real AI responses, test documents and cleanup of both work and browser environments. Reboot, repeat the checks and record 72 hours of live observations. A successful installation or local test is not production qualification.

A little help choosing the right computer

Check the computer you plan to use: on Ubuntu, open Settings → System → About; on Windows, open Settings → System → About; on a Mac, choose the Apple menu → About This Mac. Look for the system name and version. This release needs Ubuntu Desktop 24.04 LTS.

You can keep exploring on this device. Windows, macOS, WSL, other Ubuntu versions and headless servers are outside this release’s qualification target. This website does not check your device automatically. A supported architecture alone does not qualify a particular machine.

The simple tour

A place for intelligence to do useful work.

AGI Fleet is software that turns a dedicated Linux computer into an AI work environment. It is designed to connect practical digital work to a clear process for approval, review and payment.

01 / CREATE

An Agent does the work

It uses approved tools to produce something useful, such as code, documents or tests.

02 / REVIEW

A Node checks it

Independent review checks the result and evidence against the requirements.

03 / CONTROL

You keep approval

A separate approval account, called signer, protects the wallet and controls spending limits.

Where AGI Jobs fits

Fleet provides the work environment. AGIJobManager connects a work request to checks and payment rules, using USDC. The aim is useful, verifiable results with clear responsibilities.

A few questions you may have

Will clicking through this guide change my computer?

No. The guide only shows information and remembers basic choices in this browser when storage is available. Downloads require a separate click. Installing Fleet requires the steps in the installation guide, administrator preparation and your own approvals.

Do I need to understand AI or cryptocurrency to start?

No technical knowledge is needed to explore. Setting up this qualification release does require help with Ubuntu and Terminal commands. Wallet setup happens in the separate local signer application. Keep recovery words private, and leave funding and paid work off until qualification is complete.

Can I earn money by running an Agent or Node?

There is no guarantee of jobs, earnings or profit. Real operation has costs and must meet qualification, identity, policy and settlement requirements. Begin with controlled testing, an approved provider budget and no funded transactions.

What is saved when I use this guide?

Only this guide’s path, computer choice, role, current step and larger-text preference are saved locally in your browser, when available. Nothing you choose here is sent to an account or used to start work. Use “Clear choices & start again” to remove your saved journey. Larger text stays on if you chose it; use the text-size button to change it. On a shared device, clear your choices when finished.
