Have these ready

  • A dedicated Apple silicon Mac (M1 or M2), running macOS 15 or newer, with a fresh Standard work account.
  • Plan for 16 GB of memory and 40 GB of free storage. These are planning recommendations; an 8 GB Mac has not been qualified.
  • A reliable internet connection and the provider access needed for the prompted sign-ins.
  • An administrator to create the accounts, plus the pinned Apple silicon Docker Desktop version in the guide. The work account stays Standard, without administrator rights.

Know the costs and timing

  • Exploring this guide does not cost anything.
  • Your computer, internet, electricity and AI provider usage can have costs. Set your approved budget before testing.
  • Plan a supervised installation, then 72 uninterrupted hours of live observation after initial checks.
  • Paid work and earnings are not guaranteed. Keep funding and transactions off during qualification.
What setup involves — for you and your helper
Three accounts, with clear jobs. Your existing administrator prepares the computer. A new approval account (called signer) protects the wallet and approvals. A separate new work account (called compute) does the AI work. Your administrator creates these accounts in macOS System Settings. The compute account must remain Standard.
  1. Administrator · prepare the Mac

    Check the download and create the accounts

    Use the complete Mac archive and its matching checksums. Verify before extracting. Create a separate approval account and a fresh Standard work account. Read the Gatekeeper instructions with your helper: this candidate is not Developer ID signed or notarized. Do not disable macOS security controls.

  2. Signer · approval account

    Keep your wallet separate

    Open the role’s local app, create a new unfunded qualification wallet, protect its recovery words and export the private connection setup file. First launch prepares a verified private Node runtime for this account. Never put the wallet, recovery words or connection file into this website.

  3. Private transfer

    Move only the connection file

    Use the guide to transfer only the setup JSON to the Standard work account. The file must belong to the work account with owner-only permissions; follow the guide’s transfer checks. The wallet stays in the signer account; never copy its entire home folder.

  4. Compute · Standard work account

    Open the installer and follow its prompts

    Start the pinned Docker Desktop with per-user tools. Open Install.command from the extracted Fleet or matching role folder, then provide the private connection file. The installer prepares its private runtime. Follow the separate Creator, Checker and Reviewer sign-ins described in the guide. Read the policy before typing its requested approval.

  5. Both accounts · follow the guide

    Prove the complete workflow

    Confirm authenticated readiness and completed installation. Test live AI responses, example documents, both work and browser cleanup, and STOP controls. Reboot, repeat and record the required physical observations, including 72 uninterrupted hours of live endurance. A listening service or a green automated test does not qualify the Mac for funded jobs.

The simple tour

A place for intelligence to do useful work.

AGI Fleet is software that turns a dedicated Apple silicon Mac into an AI work environment. It is designed to connect practical digital work to a clear process for approval, review and payment.

01 / CREATE

An Agent does the work

It uses approved tools to produce something useful, such as code, documents or tests.

02 / REVIEW

A Node checks it

Independent review checks the result and evidence against the requirements.

03 / CONTROL

You keep approval

A separate approval account, called signer, protects the wallet and controls spending limits.

A few questions you may have

Will clicking through this guide change my computer?

No. The guide only shows information and remembers basic choices in this browser when storage is available. Downloads require a separate click. Installing Fleet requires the steps in the installation guide, administrator preparation and your own approvals.

Do I need to understand AI or cryptocurrency to start?

No technical knowledge is needed to explore. Setting up this qualification release does require help with Mac setup and Terminal commands. Wallet setup happens in the separate local signer application. Keep recovery words private, and leave funding and paid work off until qualification is complete.

Can I earn money by running an Agent or Node?

There is no guarantee of jobs, earnings or profit. Real operation has costs and must meet qualification, identity, policy and settlement requirements. Begin with controlled testing, an approved provider budget and no funded transactions.

What is saved when I use this guide?

Only this guide’s path, computer choice, role, current step and larger-text preference are saved locally in your browser, when available. Nothing you choose here is sent to an account or used to start work. Use “Clear choices & start again” to remove your saved journey. Larger text stays on if you chose it; use the text-size button to change it. On a shared device, clear your choices when finished.
